Platinum roadmap

One playthrough, no online trophies, roughly 200 hours. Work the stages in order and the platinum takes care of itself. Built from the official trophy descriptions and the in-game 100% checklist quotas.

Missables watch

GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a missed moment can mean a second playthrough. One trophy is currently flagged as potentially missable; the rest of this page is the save discipline that keeps you safe either way.

The save habit that protects the platinum

  • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — you have 15 slots, use them.
  • Save before the final mission, and before any big heist or vault job.
  • Never turn on a cheat: cheats disable trophies (and saving) while active.
  • Do Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation before the chapter ends (see Positive Mental Attitude).
  • Free roam continues after the story, so cleanup is safe — the risk is only inside chapters.

Save & settings

Your ticks and counters live in this browser's localStorage. Export them to a file to back them up or move them to another machine; import brings them back. Everything works offline.

About this guide

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published on PlayStation Network (via Exophase, cross-checked with PSNProfiles, 18 Nov 2026): 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden until earned. Every guide is written for this project in our own words, and each one names its sources. Where a hidden trophy's method is still unconfirmed at launch, the guide says so instead of guessing. No Rockstar artwork or logos are used.
